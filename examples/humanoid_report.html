02 / What we implemented

Preserve a walking policy; compose a manipulation controller

What TensorCode contributes

The supplied network is represented explicitly in the shared tensor IR, including normalization and ELU activations. We execute it symbolically, as an autograd circuit, and as a scheduled attention machine. The 31-joint PD/torque-limiting law is compiled to the same selected substrate.

ALG.2 retrieves operands through real scaled dot-product attention. The operation schedule and local arithmetic remain compiler-supplied. This is the exact executor, not the pretrained-transformer structural-bias experiment.

What we supply around it

A Cartesian inverse-kinematics controller guides the right arm over the pedestal, then into the grasp. A state machine closes the jaws, lifts, holds and releases. During manipulation, seven right-arm position and velocity channels are neutralized in the walking policy’s observation; torso and leg feedback remain measured.

The robot and object pose are known from simulation. There is no image perception, autonomous object discovery or newly trained grasp policy in this result.

04 / Resources, limitations and reproduction

Kept headroom on both machines

When unrelated host workloads drove CPU use above the guard threshold, our host simulation paused and was stopped. Final evaluation and rendering moved to the GB10 peer. The peer jobs used CPU-only inference and software rendering; GPU compute was disabled.

Active guard

Pause or stop on pressure

Pause our unit above 75% observed total CPU use; resume below 60%. Stop our unit if available memory falls below 23%. Other workloads are never stopped or reconfigured.

Simulation assumptions: the G1 has an authored parallel-jaw attachment rather than its stock dexterous hand. Robot self-collisions are disabled; floor, pedestal, finger and object contacts remain active. The root and object are free bodies. There is no object weld, runtime object-pose overwrite or hidden base support. Foot and object physics are simulated; no physical robot was operated.

Where the capability comes from

  • Unitree supplies: the G1 model and pretrained walking network, pinned to 1425b15f73bd4095f0df53709d7c389c3eb9e790 in unitree_rl_mjlab.
  • This work adds: explicit TensorCode lowering and execution, the compiled servo, a contact gripper, reach/lift sequencing, the arm-observation interface, physical controls, recorded evidence and this report.
  • No new policy training: all walking weights remain frozen. The successful grasp uses authored control and supplied state. We claim preserved behavior and a working composed task—not improved locomotion from TensorCode.
